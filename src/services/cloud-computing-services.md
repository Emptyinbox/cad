## Frequently Asked Questions

Browse some of the frequently asked questions about our cloud computing services.

### Will moving to the cloud disrupt my business operations?

Not with us. We create a detailed migration plan that minimizes downtime and ensures your team has what they need before, during, and after the transition. Most businesses experience zero operational downtime during the migration process.

### What if my team isn't very tech-savvy?

That's exactly why we're here. We handle all the setup, configuration, and training-so your staff doesn't need to become cloud experts. Our onboarding process includes user support and education tailored to your workflows.

### Is my data safe in the cloud?

Yes. We use Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365's built-in [enterprise-grade security](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/security/fundamentals/protection-customer-data) tools, combined with our own configurations and monitoring. Your data is encrypted, backed up, and protected against threats like ransomware and unauthorized access.

### Can I still access my data and systems if my team is working remotely?

Absolutely. That's one of the biggest advantages of the cloud. With Microsoft 365 and Azure, your team can securely access files, applications, and systems from anywhere.

### How do you handle backups and disaster recovery?

We implement automated, off-site cloud backups using Azure Backup and [other proven solutions](/managed-data-backups/). We test recovery processes regularly and ensure your critical data and systems can be restored quickly in case of an emergency.

### What happens if I need to scale my IT as we grow?

We build your cloud environment to scale. Whether you're adding new users, locations, or services, we can expand your infrastructure quickly-without buying new hardware or waiting weeks for provisioning.

### What's the difference between cloud hosting and Microsoft 365?

Cloud hosting refers to running your servers, databases, and business apps in Microsoft Azure. Microsoft 365 is your cloud-based productivity suite-email, Office apps, file sharing, communication, etc. We set up and manage both for a complete, secure, cloud-based IT environment.

### How much does it cost to work with Control Alt Delete?

We offer customized pricing based on your specific needs-whether it's migration, hosting, ongoing management, or all of the above. Our goal is to give you a modern, efficient IT setup at a predictable monthly rate-no surprise bills, no hidden fees.

### Do I still need an in-house IT person?

Not necessarily. Many of our clients use us as their outsourced IT department. If you have internal IT, we work alongside them to provide advanced cloud expertise, strategic planning, and daily support.
