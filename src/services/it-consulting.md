## Frequently Asked Questions

Browse some of the frequently asked questions about our IT Consulting services.

### How can IT consulting benefit my business?

IT consulting helps businesses streamline operations, improve security, reduce downtime, and optimize technology investments. It ensures your IT infrastructure supports your long-term growth goals.

### How much does IT consulting cost?

The cost depends on the scope of the project, your specific IT needs, and whether you require one-time consulting or ongoing support. We offer customized solutions to fit your budget.

### Can you help migrate my business to the cloud?

Absolutely! We specialize in cloud migrations, including moving email, data, and applications to secure, scalable cloud environments like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and other cloud solutions.

### Can you help with VPN and remote desktop setup?

Absolutely. We ensure your team can work securely and efficiently by setting up VPNs and troubleshooting remote access issues.

### How do you ensure my business stays secure from cyber threats?

We implement multi-layered security solutions, including firewalls, endpoint protection, data encryption, and cybersecurity training for employees. We also help businesses comply with Canadian data protection laws like PIPEDA and PHIPA.

### What industries do you provide IT consulting for?

We serve businesses across a variety of industries, including healthcare, legal, finance, retail, manufacturing, and more. Our IT solutions are tailored to the unique challenges of each sector.

### What's the difference between managed IT services and IT consulting?

IT consulting focuses on strategic guidance, project-based solutions, and IT planning, while **managed IT services** provide [ongoing IT support](/managed-it-support/), **maintenance, and monitoring** for your business. We offer both depending on your needs.

### Can I get IT support without signing a long-term contract?

Yes! We offer flexible consulting services, from one-time IT projects to ongoing support, so you can choose what works best for your business.
