#### Types of Network Devices Supported

Our team at Control Alt Delete has experience managing and supporting a wide range of network devices to ensure your business remains connected and secure:

- **Routers**: Configuration, optimization, and troubleshooting of [business-grade routers](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/solutions/small-business/networking/routers/index.html).
- **Switches**: Management of network switches to ensure efficient data flow and connectivity.
- **Firewalls**: Installation and maintenance of firewalls to protect against unauthorized access and cyber threats.
- **Access Points**: Setup and optimization of wireless access points for strong, reliable Wi-Fi.
- **Network Storage Devices (NAS)**: Support for [file storage](https://www.pcmag.com/picks/the-best-nas-network-attached-storage-devices) and data sharing across your network.
- **VoIP Phones**: Integration and maintenance of voice-over-IP devices for seamless communication.
- **Modems**: Troubleshooting and optimizing your connection to your Internet Service Provider.
- **Network Printers and Scanners**: Ensuring seamless integration and reliable performance for shared devices.
- **IoT Devices**: Support for smart devices connected to your business network, such as cameras and sensors.
- **Computing Devices:**And of course standard computing devices like computers, servers, laptops, and other mobile devices.
