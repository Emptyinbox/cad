# Stop being on hold with vendor support

You bought the software because it solves a real problem. But when something breaks, you're stuck on hold with a tier-1 rep reading from a script. Or you're submitting tickets that take three days to acknowledge. Or worse, the vendor blames the integration with another tool you also pay for, and now you're the project manager between two companies that don't talk to each other.

That's where we come in. Control Alt Delete acts as your single point of contact for every third-party application your business depends on — accounting tools, CRMs, line-of-business apps, design suites, e-commerce platforms, the lot. We talk to the vendors so you don't have to.

## What's covered

We support virtually any commercial software your team uses, including but not limited to:

- **Accounting and finance:** QuickBooks, Sage, Xero, FreshBooks
- **CRM and sales:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho
- **Design and creative:** Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Canva
- **Communications:** Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex
- **E-commerce and retail:** Shopify, Square, Lightspeed
- **Productivity:** Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Notion, Monday.com
- **Industry-specific tools:** practice management, dispatch, BIM, CAD, point-of-sale

If the software has a help desk, we can be the one calling it.

## What we actually do

### Vendor liaison

When a third-party tool acts up, we open the ticket, escalate it, follow up, and report back. You stay focused on your business while we sit on hold.

### License management

We track every subscription you're paying for, flag duplicate seats, catch auto-renewals before they bite, and renegotiate at renewal time when leverage exists.

### Integrations and automations

Most software pain happens at the seams between tools. We map the data flows, fix the broken integrations, and build automations (Zapier, Make, native APIs) that save your team manual work.

### Configuration and rollouts

When you adopt new software, we handle the technical setup — user provisioning, SSO, security policies, custom fields, role permissions, and admin configuration so the tool actually fits how you work.

### Training and adoption

We document the workflows, train your team, and answer "how do I do X" questions so your software investment actually gets used.

## Why this matters

The average small business pays for 30+ SaaS subscriptions and uses maybe two-thirds of them. Software sprawl is real, integration debt is real, and "shadow IT" — tools your team signed up for without telling anyone — is a security risk waiting to happen.

Putting one team in charge of every piece of third-party software in your stack means: better security, lower total cost, fewer surprise auto-renewals, and a single place to call when something breaks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you support software you've never seen before?

Yes. Most commercial software follows similar patterns for admin, security, and integration. When we hit a tool that's truly novel, we get up to speed using the vendor's documentation and developer resources, then we own it from there.

### Do you sell us software?

No. We're not a reseller and we don't earn commissions on subscriptions. Our job is to make the software you already chose work better. If something needs replacing, we'll say so honestly.

### What if our software breaks at 11pm?

Most third-party vendors don't offer 24/7 support, so we can't promise miracles. What we can do is take the ticket as soon as we open in the morning and stay on it until it's resolved.

### Is this included in managed IT support?

Routine third-party software support is included in our [Managed IT Support](/managed-it-support/) plans. Larger projects — major rollouts, complex integrations, custom automations — are quoted separately.

### How does pricing work?

Per-user, per-month, on top of your managed IT support package. We can also quote one-time projects (rollouts, migrations, integration builds) on a fixed-fee basis. [Get a quote](/contact-us/) and we'll size it based on your stack.
