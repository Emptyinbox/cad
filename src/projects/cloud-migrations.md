## How We Work

1. **Assess:** Map business priorities and current systems. We identify the highest-value and lowest-risk moves first.
2. **Plan:** Build a phased roadmap with clear owners, timelines, and what success looks like for each step.
3. **Move & Improve:** Execute in stages, keep work running, and tune costs and performance as you go.

## What You Should Ask a Potential Partner

- **How will you keep our business running while we move?** Look for a phased plan built to preserve productivity.
- **How do you decide where each workload goes?** Expect evidence-based placement, not a one-size-fits-all answer.
- **How will you give Finance clarity?** Ask how cost tracking and forecasting will be presented to leadership.
- **What changes will our people feel, and how will you help?** You want simple training, clear communications, and hands-on support.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Cloud Migrations

Browse some of the frequently asked questions about our cloud migration services.

### Is this just moving servers?

No. Server or email moves are small, tactical steps inside a broader shift in how your business runs technology, including tools, processes, and economics.

### Will our teams be disrupted?

A good plan keeps people productive while changes happen in the background. That's the benchmark to hold partners to.

### Do we have to move everything at once?

No. Most organizations migrate in phases based on value and readiness. Many keep a hybrid mix that evolves over time.

### How do we know it's worth it?

Look for visible improvements in speed, reliability, and financial transparency, outcomes leadership and Finance can measure.

### How do we control costs after go-live?

Expect a simple cadence to review usage and spend, with recommendations to right-size and avoid surprise charges.

### What does security mean here?

Using standard, well-supported tools and sensible protections reduces risk and minimizes training overhead.
