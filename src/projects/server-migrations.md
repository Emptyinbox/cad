## Frequently Asked Questions About Migrating Your Servers

Browse some of the frequently asked questions about our server migration services.

### What is a server migration, and why would my business need one?

A server migration is the process of moving your data, applications, and settings from an old server to a new one. This could be new hardware in your office or a secure cloud platform. Businesses usually need a migration when their current server is out of warranty, running outdated software, or no longer keeping up with day-to-day demands.

### Will my business experience downtime during the migration?

We plan migrations to minimize interruptions. In most cases, work can be scheduled after business hours or on weekends so your team stays productive. If short periods of downtime are unavoidable, we will explain this up front and create a plan to keep things running smoothly.

### Is there a risk of losing data?

No business wants to hear "it's gone." That is why Ctrl Alt Delete follows strict backup and testing procedures before we move anything. We secure your data, verify everything in staging, and confirm that your files, applications, and accounts come across intact.

### How much does a server migration cost?

Most small-business server migrations land between **$5,000 and $15,000 all-in**, depending on the size of your environment and whether you are moving to new on-site hardware or into the cloud. Professional services are billed at $145/hr, and we provide a fixed quote before any work begins with no hidden fees and no surprises. See our [pricing page](/pricing/) for more detail.

### Should I replace my physical server or move to the cloud?

That depends on your business goals. A new on-premises server gives you full control, while cloud hosting offers flexibility and remote access. We will walk you through the pros and cons of each option so you can make the decision that is right for your business.

### Will my existing applications still work?

Applications like QuickBooks, industry-specific tools, or point-of-sale systems are tested for compatibility before the move. Our goal is for your team to log in on Monday morning and keep working without interruption.

### What happens to my old server after migration?

We will securely retire your old server. That can mean repurposing it for non-critical tasks, wiping it for use as a backup, or recycling it responsibly, depending on what makes the most sense for your business.

### How will my new server environment be secured?

Every migration includes a review of your security setup. We make sure you have proper access controls, encryption, backups, and patch management in place so your systems stay protected and compliant with industry regulations.

### What support do you provide after the migration?

We do not just move your data and leave. Ctrl Alt Delete offers [ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and support](/server-support/) to keep your servers healthy and your business running smoothly long after the migration is complete.
