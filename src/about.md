## About Control Alt Delete

Founded in 2013 Control Alt Delete now handles IT support for almost 60 companies in Canada with over 2000 users under full management.

We're a team of 22 Canadian nice guys and gals and general IT do gooders. We have some of the brightest and most seasoned engineers in the industry.

In addition our background as "white hat" hackers means we're well ahead of the curve when it comes to cybersecurity and keeping you safe.

We have 2 offices in Whistler and Vancouver with techs distributed throughout the lower mainland to provide onsite support.
